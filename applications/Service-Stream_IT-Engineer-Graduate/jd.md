## About the job

Service Stream are excited to open applications for an **IT Engineering Graduate** to join our Group Business Information Services (GBIS/IT) division, and form part of our cohort for the **2027 Ignite Graduate Program**.

You will work across 5 core disciplines — Infrastructure Systems Engineering, Network Administration, Digital Support, End User Computing (EUC), and Applications Support — providing broad exposure to enterprise infrastructure, cloud and networking technologies, cybersecurity, device and identity management, application support, service management, and technology project delivery. This role provides a development pathway into positions such as Systems Administrator, Cloud Engineer, Cyber Security Analyst, Infrastructure Engineer and Digital Support Analyst.

## About us

Service Stream is an equal opportunity ASX-listed business that develops and operates Australia's essential services networks across telecommunications, utilities, transport, defence, and social infrastructure industries - we keep communities connected. We specialise in the design, construction, operation and maintenance of assets across these industries and have a 5,500 + strong workforce. With major contract wins this year, including with the Department of Defence, Service Stream continues to grow. As we look ahead to an even stronger 2027, there has never been a better time to join us!

## About the program

Our 2-year program is designed to support your personal and professional growth through a balanced mix of on-the-job experience, learning from others, and structured formal development.

Our program features:

- **Job rotations** – you will have the opportunity to work across different areas of specialisation and projects.
- **Networking events** – meet and greets with our executives as well as our business leaders, other graduates, and graduate alumni.
- **Professional membership** – for your first year we will give you access to your relevant association.
- **Learning courses** - to build capability in areas such as personal branding, influencing, finance and presenting.
- **Annual site visits** - providing on the job experiences and exposure to various projects across Australia.
- **Support and guidance** - through leadership support, a buddy system, a dedicated and enthusiastic graduate program team and mentoring opportunities.
- **Paid leave** - parental, cultural, community service, defence, study, corporate volunteering and purchased leave available.
- **Access** - discounts for hundreds of businesses such as Woolworths, Air BNB, The Iconic, JB HI-FI, Dan Murphys, Bupa Health Care, and Specsavers. We also offer access to novated leasing and a share matching program.

O-Week kicks off in February 2027 from our head office in Melbourne, marking your first day of employment. We will provide remuneration increases every 6 months for the duration of this program, and when you finish you will have a permanent role with us!

## About you — eligibility

To be eligible for the program, you must:

- Have completed, or about to complete, a minimum 3-year undergraduate degree such as IT, Computer Science or Network Engineering within the last 5 years from a recognised university.
- Be available to work full-time for the 2-year duration of the program from February 2027.
- **Be an Australian Citizen and able to obtain and maintain a Defence Security Clearance.**
- Hold a current Australian driver’s licence.
- Willingness to travel to Service Stream offices and operational sites as required.
