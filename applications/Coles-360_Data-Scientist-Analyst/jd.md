## About Coles 360

- Coles Group's retail media business: brands and agencies buy media across Coles, Liquorland and
  First Choice Liquor stores, websites, apps and off-network channels.
- Flybuys' data unit (Unpacked) folded into Coles 360 in 2024; rebadged **Coles 360 Empower**
  (Nov 2024) — audience insights from 9M+ active Flybuys members.
- **Coles 360 Impact** (Mar 2024) — Circana-powered measurement; closed-loop: ad exposure linked to
  till transactions.
- Data & AI team "building the data, identity and measurement foundations required to enable
  scalable retail media across owned and off-network environments".
- Leadership 2026: GM Tiffany Chen; Head of Data Intelligence and Measurement role described as
  building data science capability "from the ground up".
