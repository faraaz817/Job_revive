About One51
One51 is a specialist Data, Analytics & AI consulting firm helping organisations unlock value from their data through modern platforms, analytics, reporting, and AI-driven solutions. We partner with clients across industries to design and deliver scalable, high-impact data solutions using technologies such as Microsoft Fabric, Azure, Databricks, Snowflake, and modern cloud data platforms. At One51, we combine deep technical expertise with a pragmatic consulting approach. Our team works closely with clients to solve real business problems, modernise data environments, and enable better decision-making through trusted data and AI-driven insights.

The Role
We are looking for an innovative and technically skilled AI Engineer to join our growing consulting team. This role is ideal for someone passionate about designing, developing, and deploying AI and Generative AI solutions that create measurable business value. As an AI Engineer at One51, you will work across a range of client engagements focused on AI strategy, intelligent applications, machine learning, Generative AI, copilots, and advanced analytics solutions. You will collaborate with data engineers, analysts, consultants, architects, and client stakeholders to deliver modern AI capabilities on enterprise cloud platforms.
