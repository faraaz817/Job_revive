## About the job

We’re looking for experienced Software Engineers who enjoy solving difficult technical problems and building systems that perform reliably out in the field.

This role is ideal for engineers who like working across disciplines. Depending on your background, you might specialise in machine learning, robotics, computer vision, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, embedded software, scientific computing, or full-stack development. What matters most is strong engineering fundamentals, sound technical judgement, and the ability to build software that performs in complex real-world environments.

You’ll work as part of a multidisciplinary engineering team developing advanced autonomous systems that combine robotics, sensing, machine learning, software, and intelligent automation.

**About You:** You’re someone who enjoys understanding how complex systems work and turning difficult technical problems into elegant software solutions.
